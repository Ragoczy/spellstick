- 2026-10-06 — Faceoff (SPEC §4.1): each team's first attack-leaning runner takes it, locked 0.9 m either side of the dot; everyone else frozen at their start spots. - 2026-10-10 — Gamepad support uses the browser Gamepad API's standard layout, no new dependency. Mapping, by Xbox labels (PlayStation: A=Cross, B=Circle, X=Square, Y=Triangle): left stick or D-pad moves (analog, so a light push walks; radial deadzone 0.2); right stick aims; RT or A passes, or shoots when held and released, and takes the faceoff; LT or LB checks; X / Y / B cast spells 1–3 (the Q / E / R order); RB switches player; Menu (Start) pauses; View (Back) mutes. Both triggers and face buttons do the main actions so it plays either way, thumb on the right stick or on the buttons. Change it in `PAD_BINDINGS` in `src/ui/gamepad.ts`; the HUD and controls card read their labels from there.
- 2026-10-10 — Stick aiming: the right stick sets the aim direction; let go and you aim where you're running (twin-stick style); let go of both and the last direction stays. The sim wants an aim *point*, so a direction that crosses the goal line within 1 m of the posts aims at that spot on the goal line, pulled 0.25 m inside the post (aim assist: on net without mouse precision, and Bent Shot curves onto that spot); otherwise it aims 12 m out. This is input translation, like the AI's aim, and gives the pad no information the mouse player lacks. Tuning: `PAD_AIM_DISTANCE_M`, `AIM_ASSIST_MARGIN_M`, `AIM_INSIDE_POST_M` in `src/ui/gamepad.ts`. Input-device constants stay in `src/ui` next to the code that uses them, as the voice constants do; `src/sim/config.ts` stays game rules.
