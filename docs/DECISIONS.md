# Decisions log

Append-only. Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — decision — why — how to change`.

- 2026-10-06 — Box-lacrosse format (enclosed boards, 4 runners + goalie) — fewer players means simpler AI, the ball stays in play, and spells are more readable — change the team size in `config.ts`; switching to field lacrosse is a design change, so ask Paul.
- 2026-10-06 — Only penalty-like rules are the crease and goalie protection — canon says violence is legal; simpler — revisit after playtests.
- 2026-10-06 — Starter spells are placeholders — Paul to supply canon spell names and effects — edit `src/content/`.
- 2026-10-06 — Repo is public at `Ragoczy/spellstick`, deployed to https://ragoczy.github.io/spellstick/ — Paul chose public so GitHub Pages works on any plan — change visibility in GitHub settings (Pages on a private repo needs a paid plan).
- 2026-10-06 — Sim units are meters and seconds; origin at rink center; home team defends the −x (left) goal and attacks +x — keeps physics numbers intuitive and the renderer is a single scale factor — `src/sim/config.ts` and `src/sim/arena.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Rink is 60 × 28 m with 8 m corner radius; goal mouth 1.8 m wide, 1.0 m deep, 5 m out from the end boards; crease radius 3 m — roughly real box-lacrosse proportions, goal slightly enlarged for a top-down view — tune in `config.rink`.
- 2026-10-06 — The game clock counts integer ticks, not float seconds — no float drift, exact determinism — `periodTicksLeft` in `src/sim/match.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Fixed 1280 × 720 canvas scaled to fit the window (Phaser `Scale.FIT`) — simple, and keeps the UI scalable for later touch/mobile — `src/render/view.ts` and `src/main.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — M0 shows a temporary title/clock header drawn by Phaser; the real HUD arrives in M4 — `src/render/MatchScene.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — CI runs lint, format check, typecheck, unit tests, a 50-game sim, the build, and the Playwright smoke test before deploying; the full 1,000-game sim is run locally at each gate — keeps CI fast — `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`.
- 2026-10-06 — Prettier ignores Markdown so the docs stay exactly as written — remove `*.md` from `.prettierignore` to format them.
- 2026-10-06 — A small build tag (git SHA) sits in the bottom-right corner so playtest feedback can name the build — `src/ui/buildTag.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Movement model: velocity steers toward (input × top speed) at a capped rate (accel 32 m/s²), and bleeds off at 24 m/s² with no input; top speed 7.5 m/s, 8% slower while carrying — arcade-responsive but with a little weight and glide — `config.player`.
- 2026-10-06 — Player body radius 0.6 m and stick reach 0.95 m, a bit larger than life — at this zoom a 0.45 m player was only 9 px and the number was hard to read — `config.player.radius`, `config.player.stickReach`.
- 2026-10-06 — The stick always points at the aim point (mouse for humans); a carried ball sits at the stick head — makes aim visible before passes and shots exist — `stickHead()` in `src/sim/match.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Scooping: the nearest eligible player within 1.15 m gets one attempt per tick; success chance slides from 95% (same velocity as the ball) to 55% (≥ 9 m/s relative speed). A miss knocks the ball away and that player waits 0.3 s; a player who just released the ball waits 0.6 s — one attempt with a retry delay keeps the speed bonus meaningful (a per-tick roll would make every scoop near-certain) — `config.scoop`.
- 2026-10-06 — The ball is 2D (no height). Loose-ball friction is constant deceleration plus drag; board bounces keep 65% of normal speed and 90% of tangential speed. Fast balls substep so they can't tunnel — `config.ball`.
- 2026-10-06 — Until M2 adds scoring, goals are solid boxes for both players and the ball — `src/sim/physics.ts` (`goalBox`).
- 2026-10-06 — Debug keys: G drops the ball, T tosses it toward the cursor at 14 m/s. They're `debugDrop`/`debugToss` on the input command and will be removed or hidden before v1 — `config.debug`, `src/ui/humanInput.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Until real team AI lands in M3, the headless sim plays a 1v1 duel with a placeholder "chaser" AI: chase loose balls, mark the carrier from the goal side, and run laps when carrying — gives movement, scooping, and the stuck detector something to measure — `src/ai/chaser.ts`, `duelRoster()`.
- 2026-10-06 — "Stuck" means staying within 1 m of one spot for more than 5 s of live play; each 5 s window counts as one incident — `config.diagnostics`, `tools/stuck.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — The browser build interpolates rendered positions between sim ticks — smooth on 120/144 Hz monitors without touching the 60 Hz sim — `src/render/MatchScene.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — In the M1 practice build, the match restarts when the 10-minute clock runs out — `src/render/MatchScene.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — One button for pass and shoot, decided in the sim: commands carry `primary` (button held). Releasing within 0.18 s passes; holding longer charges a shot (full charge at 1.0 s), fired on release. A press only counts if it starts while you have the ball — the AI uses exactly the same input, and holding the button through a catch can't fire by accident — `src/sim/actions.ts`, `config.pass.tapSeconds`, `config.shot`.
- 2026-10-06 — Assist magnetism picks the teammate closest to the aim line within a 22° cone and 32 m (ties: the nearer one), leads them, and bends the pass toward their stick at up to 70°/s. SPEC says "nearest teammate in the cone"; I read that as nearest to where you aimed, since nearest-by-distance would steal passes meant for someone farther along the same line — `choosePassTarget()`, `config.pass`.
- 2026-10-06 — Passes and shots are "in flight" (light air drag, gold glow) until caught, saved, blocked, hitting something, or landing (passes 1.1 s, shots 1.4 s), then roll as ground balls. Only in-flight passes can be caught; only ground balls can be scooped — `src/sim/ball.ts`, `Flight` in `types.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Catching: anywhere along the body-to-stick-head line within 1 m. Passing team 95%→75% by relative speed; opponents intercept 60%→30%; a missed interception lets the pass sail on, a dropped catch pops loose — `config.catch`.
- 2026-10-06 — Shots: 18–32 m/s by charge; aim error ±1.5° standing, up to ±5.5° at a full run; charging slows you to 75%. Opposing runners' bodies block 60% of shots that hit them; teammates never block — `config.shot`.
- 2026-10-06 — Goalie saves are resolved by the sim (the AI only positions the goalie): a shot whose path comes within 1.05 m of the goalie is saved with chance 1.08 − 0.15·speed − 0.6·offset − 0.3·closeness (capped 5–97%), where offset is the closest approach of the shot's line to the goalie. 40% of saves are caught, the rest rebound out at 35% speed, ±65°. Measured vs the AI goalie: placed full-power shot from 9 m ≈ 30–40% goals, straight at the goalie ≈ 15%, from 5 m ≈ 50%, from 14 m ≈ 6% — `config.goalie`, `saveChance()`.
- 2026-10-06 — The goal frame is two side bars and a back bar (the ball collides with them); the mouth is open, and a goal is the ball's center crossing the goal line between the posts. Players still treat the whole goal as solid — `goalFrameSegments()`.
- 2026-10-06 — After a goal: 2 s dead-ball pause (clock stopped), then everyone resets to their start spots and the ball is placed loose at center. Faceoffs replace this in M4 — `resetPositions()` in `src/sim/match.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Exact distance ties for scoops and catches are broken with the seeded RNG. Picking by player id handed every mirrored race (e.g. the post-goal sprint to center) to the away team: the first sim run had away winning 46 of 50 — `nearestWithFairTies()`.
- 2026-10-06 — Goalies are exempt from the stuck-player check; standing in the crease while play is at the other end is their job — `tools/stuck.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Until M3's full teams, the headless sim plays 2 runners + goalie per side with the new runner/goalie AI — `SIM_RUNNERS_PER_SIDE` in `tools/runMatch.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Runner AI (base for M3): shoots with an open look within 11 m (not at hopeless angles), jukes across the goal face when covered, resets back out after ~1.25 s without a look or 1.5 s without progress, passes when stuck or when a teammate is clearly better placed, supports on the far side with constant drift, marks goal-side man-to-man with a ~10-tick reaction lag, and the nearest runner chases loose balls — `src/ai/runner.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — The headless sim runs games across worker threads (all cores but one by default; `--workers N` to override). Results are identical to a single-threaded run because each game depends only on its seed. 1,000 games: 103 s → ~23 s — `tools/sim.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Glows use normal blending, not additive: additive gold on the green floor came out olive — `src/render/BallView.ts`, `PlayerView.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — The debug toss key (T) is gone now that real passes exist; G (drop) stays for testing.
- 2026-10-06 — Body check = right click: an 11 m/s dash for 0.18 s (about 2 m) in the move direction (or where the stick points if you're not moving), 1.4 s cooldown. It hits the first opponent within two body radii + 0.3 m: they're knocked back 8 m/s and staggered 0.6 s (no control, no stick, any shot wind-up lost); a carrier loses the ball 40% of the time. The checker keeps 30% of their speed. One hit per dash; teammates are never hit — `src/sim/checks.ts`, `config.check`.
- 2026-10-06 — SPEC §4.5 mentions both body and stick checks, but §5 has one check button. v1 has one check that covers both (the ball-loosening part is the "stick check") — add a second input if Paul wants them distinct.
- 2026-10-06 — Board slams: a staggered player who hits the boards faster than 3 m/s is staggered 0.4 s longer (and the camera shakes) — canon says hits into the boards are legal, so they should feel like something — `config.check.boardSlam*`.
- 2026-10-06 — Goalie protection (SPEC §4.6): a goalie within their own crease circle can't be hit; the checker bounces back at 5 m/s and is staggered 0.4 s. A goalie outside the crease can be hit — `goalieProtected()`.
- 2026-10-06 — All checks landing on the same tick are found first and applied together, so mutual checks both land. Resolving them in player order let the home team (lower ids) win every simultaneous collision — `resolveChecks()`.
- 2026-10-06 — Runner leans: half the runners (rounded down) lean attack, the rest defense (4 → 2 + 2, 3 → 1 + 2, 5 → 2 + 3). Attack-leaners start near center and work the near wings on offense; defense-leaners start deep and play the high slots — `runnerLeans()`, `scrimmageRoster()`, `offenseSpot()`.
- 2026-10-06 — Team defense: base man-to-man pairs our defense-leaners with their attack-leaners (and vice versa), ordered across the floor; the defender nearest the carrier always picks up the carrier and swaps marks with whoever had them. Off-ball defenders play goal-side of their man shaded 20% toward the ball, and sag to the crease (along their man's line, shaded toward the ball) when their man is more than 16 m from goal — `src/ai/tactics.ts`, `defense()` in `src/ai/runner.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — Team offense: off-ball runners hold a spread spot with a constant drift (±3.5 m over ~4.5 s), step away from defenders within 2.5 m, and attack-leaners cut to the crease edge for ~0.9 s every 4 s. The carrier sidesteps around a defender in front of them (leaning straight away from pressure cancelled their drive and stalled play), and sometimes shoots through traffic — `offBallOffense()`, `carrier()`.
- 2026-10-06 — AI steering post-process: every AI move is nudged away from teammates closer than 2.2 m and slid around goal frames (a runner got stuck pushing into the back of its own net) — `src/ai/steering.ts`.
- 2026-10-06 — All AI tuning moved into `config.ai`: `levels.easy/normal/hard` (reaction time, aim error, shoot range, pass-lane caution, check eagerness, goalie reaction) plus shared `runner` and `goalie` behavior numbers, per CLAUDE.md's no-magic-numbers rule. Faceoff timing joins the levels in M4. Measured (40 games each): hard beats easy 36–2, normal beats easy 33–4, hard beats normal 21–11 — `config.ai`.
- 2026-10-06 — AI check eagerness is a per-tick chance while in range (normal 0.4%, i.e. about once every 4 s of close pressure), 4× higher against a carrier winding up a shot. The first full-team run checked every ~2 s and the ball never settled (126 possession changes a game) — `checkChancePerTick`, `checkWindupMultiplier`.

Space switches to the teammate nearest the ball (or the next nearest if that's already you) when your team doesn't have it. Your marker turns grey while your check is on cooldown — `src/render/MatchScene.ts`.
